How to Use

Getting Started

Welcome! Follow these simple steps to set up your financial overview and track your everyday spending effortlessly.

1. Budget & Reports

Set up your core targets and recurring monthly costs to build a baseline for your finances:

  • Set budget and savings goals: Establish monthly spending limits and targeted savings goals.
  • Add fixed expenses: Enter regular monthly bills like rent, subscriptions, or utilities.

2. Transactions Page

Keep your records accurate by logging daily activity as it happens:

  • Log daily transactions: Record everyday income and expenses with just a few taps.
  • Use transaction presets: Save frequent micro-expenses—such as daily bus fares or morning coffee—for one-click logging.

Adding Transactions

To keep your insights reliable, log income and expenses promptly. Categorize each entry accurately (e.g., Food, Transportation, Utilities) and use custom notes or transaction presets to log routine purchases in seconds.

Managing Your Budget

Review your budget periodically in the Reports section to monitor spending habits against your monthly limits. Use visual spending breakdowns to identify trends, adjust monthly thresholds, and ensure you remain on target to hit your savings goals.
